Week-by-week recipe

Condensed weekly plan. Cap 8–10 hr/week; cut during exams.

Constraint

Full course load. Cap at 8–10 hours/week in 2–3 focused sessions (e.g. Tue 90 · Thu 90 · Sat 4–5). Midterms/finals = 2–4 hr max or pause. Phone in another room. One tab. Timer on.

Father Amazon referral

Get the referral submitted before you hit Apply on 10552937. Send resume + job link + 3 bullet “why me” in one email; don’t apply until he confirms it’s in.
