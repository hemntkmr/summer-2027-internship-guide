Talk with Dad

Read aloud together (~20–25 min). Includes Sunday script and closing checklist.

How to use

Print this or keep it on your phone. Read it aloud in a calm voice. Pause where it says (pause). You’re not lecturing — you’re agreeing on a simple plan you both can see. ~20–25 minutes the first time. Then 10 minutes every Sunday with the tracker.

Opening (2 minutes)

Dad

“I’ve been putting a plan together so you’re not guessing what ‘prep for Amazon and Microsoft’ means. We’re going to keep it light on hours—about eight to ten hours a week, in a few focused blocks—so school still comes first. When midterms or finals hit, we drop to two to four hours or we pause. Grades win.” (pause)

“I’ll help with one big thing: the Amazon referral. You do the resume, the projects, the practice, and the applications. We’ll use a shared tracker so ‘done’ always means there’s a link I can open—not just a vibe. Sound fair?” (pause for yes)

Part 1 — How we’ll work together

Dad

“Every week we’ll use the same little log. You pick a few tasks from the recipe. For each one you mark Done, you drop a proof link—a resume PDF, a screenshot that you applied, a GitHub plus a short demo, a list of LeetCode problems you finished. If there’s no link, it’s not Done. That’s the whole rule.” (pause)

“My job on Sunday is simple. I open the week’s table, click every Done link once, and if something’s missing I text you one line: ‘Need proof link for [that task].’ Then I stop. I’m not grading your code. I’m just keeping us honest.”

Part 2 — What your resume needs

Dad

“You’re applying for a first software internship. You don’t need five jobs. You need a one-page resume: education, maybe one experience line if you have EPICS or research, and two solid projects—three max. Every strong bullet looks like: what you built, with what tools, and a number—time saved, users, accuracy, speed.” (pause)

“We’re not copying anyone else’s résumé. We’re copying the shape: short, quantified, cloud or agent projects named honestly, robotics framed as software. You’ll pick one main project—ROS, vision, or a small campus app—and maybe a thin AWS piece so Seattle recruiters see cloud. Publish it on GitHub with a short demo. Put LinkedIn and GitHub in the header.”

This week’s ask (say it plainly):

“By next Sunday I want resume version one as a PDF in the shared folder, and a note on which two projects you’re building.”

Part 3 — Where you learn what they ask

Dad

“When I prepped people for Amazon, the useful places were forums where candidates write what they were asked and paste solutions. Bookmark these three first:

  1. LeetCode Discuss — leetcode.com/discuss
  2. TeamBlind — teamblind.com
  3. Glassdoor — glassdoor.com/Interview

Reddit’s fine too—r/csMajors, r/leetcode—but start with those three. Practice live with a friend on Pramp when you’re closer to interviews. Don’t pay for fancy banks until the free stuff is a habit.”

Part 4 — The calendar in plain English

Dad

“Here’s the season in one breath.” (pause)

  • October into mid-November: resume from scratch, my Amazon referral before you hit Apply on job 10552937, then Amazon, NVIDIA, Microsoft when a student req is live, start Caterpillar.
  • Caterpillar deadline is December 13—don’t miss that.
  • Eight to ten hours a week—Tuesday and Thursday short sessions, Saturday the long one. Phone in another room.
  • If exams are this week, career work shrinks. We don’t burn GPA for one more application.
  • January: Purdue Co-op Days and more apps as they open.
  • Through April: keep one project shipping, keep LeetCode steady, show up for OAs and interviews when they come.

“Seattle-first—Amazon, Microsoft—but we’re not Seattle-only. NVIDIA, Caterpillar, Lockheed, Meta if it’s open: apply when the door is open.”

Part 5 — The Amazon rule (say slowly)

Say this slowly

“For Amazon: you send me your resume PDF, the job link for 10552937, and three short bullets on why you. I submit the referral. You wait until I say it’s in. Then you apply. Not before. If I’m slow more than about five days, we talk and you may apply anyway so you don’t miss the rolling window—but the default is referral first.”
