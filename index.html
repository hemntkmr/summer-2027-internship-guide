The deal

This guide turns “prep for Amazon and Microsoft” into a calm, ordered plan. Dad helps with the Amazon referral and a Sunday proof check. You do the resume, projects, practice, and applications. School always wins.

Amazon rule — memorize this

Job 10552937: send Dad your resume PDF + job link + three “why me” bullets. He submits the referral first. You wait until he says it’s in. Then you apply. If he’s slow more than ~5 days, talk — you may apply anyway so you don’t miss the rolling window. Default = referral first.

How to use this (start now)

  1. Talk with Dad — read the walkthrough aloud once (~20–25 min), then use the Sunday script weekly.
  2. Set up tracking — shared Drive folder + weekly log; Done only with a link Dad can open.
  3. Build resume v0 → v1 — one page, two projects, Verb + Tech + Metric bullets.
  4. Follow the week-by-week recipe — Oct 2026 through Apr 2027, cut hours during exams.
  5. Apply in order — Amazon (post-referral), NVIDIA, Microsoft when live, Caterpillar by Dec 13, then the rest.

Standing rules

  • Hours: Cap career work at 8–10 hr/week in 2–3 blocks (e.g. Tue 90 min · Thu 90 min · Sat 4–5 hr).
  • Exams: Midterms / finals weeks = 2–4 hr max or pause. Grades beat applications.
  • Sessions: Phone in another room. One tab. Timer on. Stop when the week’s hours are done.
  • Seattle-first, not Seattle-only: Amazon & Microsoft priority; also NVIDIA, Caterpillar, Lockheed, Meta when open.
